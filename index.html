A plain scroll means zoom from a mouse and pan from a trackpad, and the browser does not say which one you have. The page guesses from the shape of the events; if it guesses wrong, the Wheel: button above the drawings pins it to zoom or to pan and remembers the choice. Pinch and ⌘/Ctrl+scroll always zoom.

Drawings are produced by the classical, no-AI reader — the code that ships. No trained model is involved, so judge the drawing, not a model.

Everything you type is written to this browser straight away, so a refresh or a closed tab loses nothing. It lives only in this browser though — export the CSV before you switch machine or clear your history.
